Our Services

Focusing on Biodiversity Services

Comprehensive solutions in Indonesia Archipelago.

Feasibility Analysis

Our team of experts can help whether your project plan is feasible or not regarding to the biodiversity aspect.

Screening & Scoping

Conduct screening and scoping of project impact to make your future survey and assessment more effective and efficient.

Rapid Survey

Conduct terrestrial, freshwater and marine waters biodiversity survey following scientific method and approach.

Detailed Survey

Detailed study needed by the project following scientific method and approach as well as specific requirements.

Impact Assessment (EIA)

Support in developing Environmental Impact Assessment based on national and international requirements.

Action & Management Plan

Develop management, strategy, and action plan including site-level SOP regarding biodiversity protection.

Critical Habitat Assessment

Assessing critical habitats as a consequence of the presence of significant biodiversity value in your site.

Ecosystem Services

Conduct ecosystem services assessment including mitigation measures based on Millennium Ecosystem Assessment.

Designing Management

Design each project phase including BAP, BMP and Biodiversity Offset Management Plan (BOMP).

Monitoring & Audit

Routine monitoring and internal audit for your national and international obligation.

Implementation Program

Support your project obligation in implementing biodiversity management and monitoring program.

Consultation & Liaison

Support consultation and liaison with specific stakeholders including NGO and environmentalists.

Training & Awareness

Increase project team knowledge, understanding and capability on biodiversity.

Second Man

BCS expert can assist your project team on site regarding project obligation and implementation.
